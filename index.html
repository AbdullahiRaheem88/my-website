Smallvest Limited is a business growth consulting firm that combines strategic advisory, professional training, and AI-powered systems to help organisations and individuals scale with confidence.

We operate at the intersection of human expertise and intelligent automation — not as a futurist concept, but as a practical, deployed reality in the businesses we work with.

Founded in Nigeria. Operating internationally. Built for Africa first.

The Founder

Abdullahi Raheem
Founder & Principal Consultant, Smallvest Limited

Abdullahi is a business growth consultant, AI practitioner, and product builder with a track record spanning Nigeria, Ghana, the UK, and the US. He combines deep expertise in project management, operations, and digital strategy with a practical approach to AI adoption that delivers results businesses can measure.

He has collaborated with GIZ on last-mile logistics innovation across four African countries, developed capacity-building frameworks for 100,000 MSMEs, and trained and mentored more than 3,000 project managers through one-on-one sessions and global bootcamp programmes.

At Smallvest, he leads consulting engagements, builds and ships digital products — including MarketMuse HQ, HomeFind, and Alkebulan — and runs the Partner Programme that turns professional communities into revenue-generating affiliate networks.

He was also a mentor at the 2025 Code4Privacy Hackathon by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
